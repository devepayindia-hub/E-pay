Additional Smart Features

You can make your CRM stand out by adding:

One-click WhatsApp Web integration.
Email templates with placeholders like {Customer Name}, {Telecaller Name}, {Office Name}.
Automatic greeting based on time (Good Morning/Afternoon/Evening).
Bulk messaging for multiple leads.
Scheduled messages (send later).
Communication history timeline showing every call, WhatsApp, email, and SMS in one place.
AI-generated replies and follow-up messages.
Read receipts (where supported).
Automatic reminders if a lead hasn't been contacted within a defined period.
One-click sharing of brochures, price lists, franchise proposals, PDFs, videos, and location maps.

These features create a complete Omnichannel Communication Center, allowing telecallers to manage all customer interactions without leaving the CRM.
