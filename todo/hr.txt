⭐ One Feature I Strongly Recommend

Since your CRM already includes HR, Telecalling, Finance, Technical Support, Gallery, Digital Marketing, Developers, Franchise, Inventory, and Admin, add a Company Productivity Command Center.

This will be a single dashboard for the CEO, Directors, and HR Head displaying:

Live employee count by status (Working, Break, Meeting, Offline).
Office-wise and department-wise productivity.
Daily, weekly, monthly, and custom date KPIs.
Attendance, working hours, idle time, and task completion.
Revenue generated per employee and per department.
Active projects and project health.
AI-generated alerts for delays, burnout risks, underperforming teams, and resource shortages.
Leaderboards for top performers across all departments.
Executive MIS reports with drill-down from company → office → department → team → employee.

This type of command center transforms the HR module from a traditional HR system into an enterprise-grade Workforce Intelligence Platform, giving leadership real-time visibility into the organization's operational health.
