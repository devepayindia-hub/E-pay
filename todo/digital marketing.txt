Bonus Features

To make your CRM stand out, you can also add:

🤖 AI Campaign Optimizer (suggests budget changes based on ROAS).
📈 Competitor Analysis (track competitors' websites and ads).
🔥 Trending Keywords (Google Trends integration).
🎯 Lead Quality Score (Hot, Warm, Cold).
📱 Social Media Inbox (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn messages in one place).
📅 Content Approval Workflow (Marketing → Manager → Publish).
🎥 AI Image & Video Generator for ad creatives.
🔔 Alerts when campaigns overspend or underperform.
📍 Office-wise marketing performance comparison.
🏆 Monthly leaderboard for digital marketers based on leads, ROAS, content output, and campaign success.

This design gives your Digital Marketing team a complete command center for planning, executing, monitoring, and optimizing every aspect of online marketing from a single CRM dashboard.
