⭐ Additional Smart Features

To make your CRM stand out from standard helpdesk systems:

QR Code Asset Scanning: Scan a laptop or printer QR code to instantly view its service history.
Voice Notes: Engineers can record a voice note instead of typing long issue descriptions.
Photo Upload: Attach before/after repair photos.
Digital Signature: Employee signs after confirming the issue is resolved.
Auto Work Timer: Starts when the engineer begins work and stops when completed.
Location Check-in: Record which office or branch the engineer visited.
Offline Mode: Allow engineers to create work logs without internet; sync later.
Recurring Issue Alerts: Notify managers when the same employee, asset, or department reports the same issue multiple times.
Personal Work Timeline: A chronological timeline of every task, ticket, and direct support entry completed by the engineer, creating a complete history of their work for performance reviews and audits.
