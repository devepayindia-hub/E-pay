Examples:

Owner: Full access to everything.
Co-Owner: Operational and financial access.
Investor: Can view investments, profit sharing, and reports but cannot manage daily operations.
Gallery Manager: Can manage employees, sales, inventory, and customers but cannot change ownership or financial ratios.
View Only: Read-only access.
Validation Rules

The CRM should automatically validate:

Total ownership percentage = 100%
Total profit sharing percentage = 100%
Total loss sharing percentage = 100% (if applicable)
Prevent duplicate owner records for the same gallery unless intentionally added with a defined share.
Maintain a complete audit trail for every ownership, investment, or ratio change.
Allow effective dates for future ownership changes.

This mapping allows:

✅ One owner to own multiple galleries.
✅ One gallery to have multiple owners.
✅ Different capital contributions for each gallery.
✅ Different profit-sharing percentages for each gallery.

This many-to-many ownership model is scalable and is the recommended design for an enterprise-grade Franchise CRM/ERP. It supports future expansion to hundreds or thousands of galleries while maintaining accurate financial records, profit distribution, and owner-level access control.

Advanced Enterprise Features
📈 Portfolio Dashboard

Shows the owner's combined investment, profits, ROI, liabilities, and performance across all galleries.

💹 Dynamic Profit Sharing

Profit-sharing percentages can be changed with an effective date. Historical distributions remain unchanged, while future calculations use the updated ratios.

🔄 Share Transfer & Buyout

Supports selling ownership stakes between partners or bringing in new investors, with automatic ledger entries and ownership history.

📊 Multi-Gallery Consolidated Reporting

Owners can view consolidated financials across all galleries or filter reports by a single gallery, region, city, or state.

💼 Investor Dashboard

Provides investors with a read-only view of investments, returns, profit distributions, agreements, and portfolio growth without exposing operational controls.
