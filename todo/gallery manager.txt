📍 Multi-Level Approval Workflow

Gallery Managers can create purchase requests, salary changes, or expense approvals, but final approval can be routed to the Gallery Owner, Finance Manager, or Head Office based on configurable approval limits.

Visual merchandising displays

Track:

Sales targets

🤖 AI Business Assistant

Provide proactive recommendations such as:

Predicting stock shortages based on sales trends.
Highlighting abnormal expenses.
Identifying top-performing employees.
Forecasting monthly revenue.
Suggesting optimal reorder quantities.
Recommending promotions for slow-moving inventory.
Detecting unusual operational patterns that require manager attention.

With these modules, the Gallery Manager becomes the complete operational head of their assigned gallery while remaining within the permissions defined by the Gallery Owner and Head Office. This design is suitable for managing hundreds or even thousands of franchise locations from a centralized ERP/CRM platform.
